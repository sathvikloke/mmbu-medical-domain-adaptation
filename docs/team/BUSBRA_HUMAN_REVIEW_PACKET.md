## Why this is the next gate

BUS-BRA is the strongest current source candidate because one patient-grouped corpus can support global breast
ultrasound labels, lesion-grounded labels, and localization. Its archive, metadata closure, patient grouping, decoded
image/mask structure, exact-duplicate screen, perceptual triage, and deterministic transform are already receipted.
The remaining questions require accountable human judgment. More automated checks cannot establish that a lesion mask
is medically complete, that a pathology/BI-RADS target is appropriate for the proposed visual task, that two cases have
distinct provenance, or that the team accepts the attribution, disclosure, and public-checkpoint consequences.

## Frozen review population

The medical population is the union of:

1. a deterministic patient-exclusive plan with 35 records requested per pathology × BI-RADS stratum, generated by
   `rarest-pathology-birads-stratum-then-record-id-case-exclusive-v1`; it contains 199 records and has digest
   `71281236f41d658b1985906c872896647f773f6598947b526221509d34a5b912`; and
2. every record named by the structural contract's 44 box exceptions and four dimension exceptions, plus
   `bus_0110-l` and `bus_0536-r` from the perceptual-provenance candidate.

The union contains exactly 244 records. The mandatory cases cannot be replaced by easier examples. A later review may
add records to investigate a failure cluster, but may not silently remove a frozen record or reuse a case across
decision roles.

## Separate decisions and qualified roles

| Decision | Minimum accountable role | Allowed result |
|---|---|---|
| Visual and clinical semantics | Human reviewer experienced with breast ultrasound; an independent second reviewer handles every disagreement and material exception | approve exact task/field, narrow it, exclude record/task, or unresolved |
| Mask completeness and lesion policy | Same two-reviewer path; material disagreement requires independent adjudication | approve mask-derived geometry, exclude, or unresolved |
| Cases 110 and 536 provenance | Human data steward or source-author evidence; visual similarity alone is insufficient | distinct lineage, linked lineage, exclude/co-assign, or unresolved |
| Rights, purpose, attribution, disclosure, and trained-weight release | Human team authority who has reviewed the exact Zenodo version, CC BY 4.0 obligations, challenge disclosure, and planned Hugging Face release | approve named purposes, narrow, decline, or unresolved |

An AI review, clean decoder run, citation, public download, repository license, or deadline cannot fill any of these
roles. A missing reviewer identity, qualification, date, evidence locator, or decision remains blocking.

## Medical review form

For every frozen record, retain the record ID, case ID, source pathology, source BI-RADS, image dimensions, mask,
source box, and mask-derived half-open box. Record each answer separately:

1. Is the image an interpretable breast ultrasound view for the proposed task?
2. Does the mask identify the intended lesion, and is its boundary usable for the claimed localization target?
3. Is there evidence of an additional lesion or incomplete annotation that makes single-target detection or grounded
   classification invalid?
4. Are the pathology and BI-RADS fields correctly joined to this case and appropriate as source-authoritative targets?
   The reviewer need not re-diagnose biopsy truth from pixels, but must reject a broken join or unsupported task use.
5. Does the image reveal a crop, overlay, caliper, panel, or acquisition artifact that could create a non-medical
   shortcut? If yes, name the permitted task or exclusion policy; do not erase the artifact.
6. Are `modality=ultrasound` and `body_part=breast` supported? Leave submodality, specimen, stain, and any finer context
   unknown unless the source record explicitly supports them.
7. Final record disposition: eligible for named tasks, eligible only after a named correction, excluded, or unresolved.

Every `bus_0845-s` review must explicitly adjudicate the material source-box versus mask-extent discrepancy. The source
box remains QA evidence; the mask-derived box is the only proposed localization transform. All other box exceptions
must confirm that the apparent one-pixel convention does not hide a different clinical target.

## Purpose and release form

The rights/release reviewer must answer each item explicitly rather than signing a blanket “open data” statement:

- exact source: BUS-BRA version 1.0, Zenodo record 8231412, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.8231412`;
- exact archive: 133,917,740 bytes, MD5 `1f8b2be6476d58fc97bfb5e5a1ea9bab`;
- recorded license evidence: Zenodo API identifier `cc-by-4.0`;
- permitted purposes: training, development, untouched confirmation, public-weight release, and challenge submission are
  five separate yes/no decisions;
- attribution text and placement for the model card, repository, technical report, and challenge disclosure;
- whether source-derived labels, masks, boxes, and trained weights may be redistributed, and under what notices;
- whether the proposed public Hugging Face checkpoint and its disclosure are acceptable; and
- any exclusion, retention, deletion, or access-control requirement.

This packet records facts and questions, not legal advice. Uncertainty narrows or blocks the purpose; it never becomes
permission by default.

## Promotion rule

BUS-BRA advances only when all four decision rows above are complete and mutually consistent. The admitted manifest
must bind exact eligible record IDs, patient groups, tasks, context fields, exclusions, attribution, role assignment,
review evidence, and release obligations. Any unresolved record is excluded or keeps the affected task blocked.

Even after admission, training remains a separate prospective action. The first run must use the frozen external-only
B2 lane, never MMBU public examples or option vocabularies, and must preserve a lineage-disjoint untouched role.
