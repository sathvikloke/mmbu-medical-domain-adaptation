## Purpose

The full-corpus audit found four incorrect source-declared heights, 44 stored-box discrepancies, and one unresolved
perceptual cross-case candidate. This contract resolves how software must transform those facts without guessing a
medical correction or quietly turning an engineering decision into scientific approval.

The contract is generated by `tools/busbra_transform_contract.py` from two authenticated local receipts. It refuses a
tampered receipt, the wrong archive identity, incomplete exception populations, exact-duplicate failures, automatic
merge/exclusion decisions, or any receipt that claims training or model-use authority.

## Binding transform

- Decoded image dimensions are canonical. Source `Width` and `Height` are QA evidence only.
- A decoded image and mask must have identical dimensions.
- A localization target, if a later purpose review permits one, is the tight half-open `xywh` extent derived
  deterministically from nonzero mask pixels. The source `BBOX` is QA evidence only.
- `Case` is the independent unit. Every view from a case stays in one role.
- Source `valid_1` through `valid_10` fields are prohibited. The only admitted source split field is outer `kFold`.
- Cases `110` and `536` must be assigned to the same split until provenance review resolves their perceptual-hash
  candidate. Co-assignment is a leakage precaution, not a claim that the cases share a patient.

The emitted contract binds all 1,875 records and 1,064 cases, lists all four dimension exceptions and all 44 box
exceptions, and links the exact corpus and triage receipt digests.

## What this closes

This closes the software-level ambiguity that previously made any BUS-BRA transform non-reproducible: a future intake
cannot choose between source dimensions and decoded dimensions, source boxes and mask boxes, unsafe validation flags,
or ad hoc treatment of the unresolved pair.

It does **not** establish that a mask is medically correct, that every lesion is completely annotated, that the source
is independent of MMBU, that reuse and trained-weight release are approved, or that any record may enter training.
Accordingly, the contract records `structural_transform_clearance=true` while retaining
`medical_correctness_reviewed=false`, `source_proximity_reviewed=false`, `rights_and_purpose_approved=false`,
`training_ready=false`, and `model_use_authorized=false`.

## Remaining promotion gates

BUS-BRA can advance beyond quarantine only after all of the following are independently evidenced:

1. medical review of task semantics, mask completeness, multi-lesion policy, and the material `bus_0845-s` discrepancy;
2. cross-source proximity and patient/acquisition provenance review, including cases `110` and `536`;
3. source-specific rights, purpose, attribution, disclosure, and trained-weight-release decisions; and
4. an admission receipt binding the approved tasks, fields, records, groups, and release path.

Until then, the contract is executable policy for a future transform—not authorization to execute model training.
