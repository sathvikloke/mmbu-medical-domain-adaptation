# Cold independent MMBU audit — post-repair

**Audit date:** October 26, 2026  
**Audited revision:** `6344a46`  
**Method:** fresh read-only review in a separate chat, without inheriting prior ratings  
**Verdict:** **45/100; pivot the project, do not replace the repository**

This score is an evidence-state rating, not a probability of winning. The audit retained the repository's engineering,
provenance, evaluator, and failure-preservation framework, but rejected any claim that the current checkout is already
a novel learner, validated biomedical model, selected competition entry, or demonstrated research contribution.

The defensible project today is a rigorous fail-closed experimentation platform plus a narrow, unconfirmed hypothesis
about answer/context co-training and independent-unit normalization. Further governance or prose alone cannot lift the
evidence-backed score beyond approximately 55/100; scientific and competition evidence must do that.

## Evidence boundary

- The current tracked checkout and freshly executed CPU suites support software correctness and bounded mechanics.
- Tracked B1/B2 receipts support native A100 loading, backward, one-step update, adapter export, and offline reload only.
- The replicated biomedical response screen remains a historical lead: raw predictions, receipts, execution manifest,
  and analysis artifacts are absent from this checkout, and the report has zero selection or promotion authority.
- Six Stage A directories are machine-local and untracked. Their checked-in inventory establishes drift detection only.
- No external trust key, signature, identity proof, revocation record, qualified clinical review, or admitted real-source
  purpose authorization is present.
- No MMBU public-set result, official loader result, upload, or submission is claimed.

## Critical findings

1. **No executable competition candidate exists.** The authoritative phase remains blocked, candidate and track fields
   are null, and the current context endpoint is deliberately blocked because it is constant and image-independent.
2. **The only substantive biomedical performance report is not independently auditable from this checkout.** It cannot
   select a model, establish a biomedical gain, or support confirmation.

## High findings

1. Historical acquisition and structural-opening records are not present scientific authority.
2. The official-equivalent prompt, parser/judge behavior, loader/runtime envelope, multi-label behavior, packaging, and
   submission receipt process remain unresolved.
3. Learner-method novelty is not established; the strongest novelty candidate is an unconfirmed system interaction.
4. Track 3 is only an administrative provisional direction while the proposed 9B study is Track 2; neither presently
   governs an eligible model.
5. The strongest evidence state is not portable: the branch is far ahead of the remote baseline, important experimental
   artifacts are remote-only, and local Stage A roots are untracked.

## Important findings

- The network guard is an in-process socket patch rather than a complete operating-system sandbox.
- The default historical smoke/status surface is stale and can confuse operators.
- Singleton accumulation is the admitted dual-objective reference because heterogeneous-batch equivalence failed.
- Image-dependent paths were not freshly reproduced on the audit machine because optional Pillow/Torch dependencies
  were absent.
- Human-impact claims remain hypothetical without qualified clinician evaluation.
- Governance complexity is now a delivery risk relative to the lack of admissible scientific evidence.

## Strongest genuine assets

- unusually strong negative-test and failure-preservation discipline;
- honest separation of synthetic mechanics, exploratory evidence, official evaluation, and clinical validity;
- real bounded native load/backward/export/reload feasibility;
- careful evaluator arithmetic, immutable raw-output design, and parse/validate/judge separation;
- strong leakage controls and no evidence of adaptive use of MMBU public examples.

## Score ceilings

- learner novelty: **3/10** until a genuinely different mechanism is motivated by an observed residual;
- system novelty: **4.5/10** until the frozen factorial, exposure-equalized null, duplication-invariance test, and
  independent replication run;
- scientific validity: **5/10** until admitted multi-family data, valid grouping, qualified review, and untouched
  confirmation exist;
- empirical evidence: **4/10** until remote raw artifacts are imported and verified, then **6/10** until independent
  confirmation succeeds;
- rule fit: **6/10** until the final competition protocol and track eligibility are bound;
- overall: **55/100** from engineering or prose alone.

## Evidence-backed next sequence

1. Make the evidence state portable: preserve the authority-repair commits and import the remote response-screen
   receipts, raw predictions, execution manifest, and analysis under their recorded hashes.
2. Resolve and hash the external competition contract: final context prompt, parser/judge, loader/runtime, Track 3
   parameter rule, adapter packaging, and submission receipt behavior.
3. Replace the constant context target with an image-dependent, per-record, nonconstant endpoint under qualified
   independent review.
4. Obtain authentic external authority, rights, reviewer qualification, grouping, QA, and purpose evidence for the
   smallest viable multi-family source portfolio.
5. Run one prospectively frozen decision-bearing study: unchanged base, task-only LoRA, and dual-objective LoRA;
   identical admitted records; three frozen seeds; source-family holdout; preregistered uncertainty; portable raw
   artifacts. Do not add projector complexity before this test.
6. Select exactly one candidate and primary track only from that study, then rehearse the exact official loader in a
   fresh offline environment.

## Decision

**Pivot, do not replace.** Retain the evaluation and evidence framework. Stop counting governance completion as
research progress. The next score-changing work is portable, admitted, prospective biomedical evidence—not another
policy layer or rewritten project pitch.
