## Interpretation

The project is not a 9.5/10 completed entry. It is now a strong research and competition **system scaffold** with
excellent audit infrastructure and bounded native-model feasibility evidence, but no biomedical performance result.
Averaging the table would be misleading because the empirical score is a hard dependency: a zero-biomedical-result
project cannot be made competition-ready by adding more prose. The October 18
executability audit also showed that detailed prose is not an executable experiment until every scientific choice,
dependency, role, cap, and decision function is literal and hash-bound.

The earlier 8.1 MOSAIC novelty estimate was wrong. The current rank-deficient grid earns only 4.5 competition value
and 5.5 concept novelty; a genuinely crossed future design must be rescored from zero evidence and does not inherit the
older headline. QUILT's later 7.3/6.1 estimates are also obsolete. The independent adverse review plus direct
medical-VLM paraphrase-consistency literature reduce QUILT to 3.5 concept novelty and 3.0 paper-method novelty. This
does not rehabilitate MOSAIC; it means there is currently no evidence-backed 9/10 novelty idea in the portfolio.

The three-way [independent arbitration](INDEPENDENT_IDEA_ARBITRATION.md) replaces “B2 plus curriculum” with
dual-objective answer-and-context adaptation. A later exact search found that CVPR 2022 already uses
characteristic-function frequency supervision to encode count, position, and subregion counts, demoting MRFSS to 5.2
concept and 4.5 prospective paper novelty. A further exact search found region inclusion–exclusion/additivity counting,
demoting WRLVT to 4.8/4.0. None of these scores is evidence that a mechanism works.

A second orthogonal search then rejected scene-language compilers, acquisition-provenance factorization, and redundant
semantic codewords against direct scene-graph, biomedical factorization, region-grounding, and error-correcting/token
antecedents. The scoped pre-evidence **learner-mechanism** search is closed with zero survivors. Costly mechanism work
reopens only after a qualifying residual: either a prospectively frozen repeated B2/B4 residual on admitted, non-public
external data, or a precisely cited independently replicated residual matching the task, modality, and constraints with a
predeclared reason B2 cannot address it. Low-cost system, data-objective, and evaluation ideation remains open and
cannot alter frozen Lane 1 or consume protected evidence.

The later factorial/Möbius update-decomposition search also does not raise the ceiling. Task Arithmetic already owns
three-corner analogy completion, while compositional merging and functional non-additivity audits cover the key
ingredients. Its 5.8 concept-novelty result is retained only as a gauge-safe interaction diagnostic for MOSAIC; see
[the binding adverse review](FACTORIAL_UPDATE_ADVERSE_REVIEW.md).

The rules-permitted dual-vision-encoder option is now bounded as a post-B2 ablation rather than a new flagship. Its
competition upside is prospectively 7.4 but readiness is only 5.6, and its method novelty is 2.6 because multi-encoder
fusion and distillation are established. It cannot delay the base-architecture-preserving lane or raise any current
headline score; loader compatibility still requires organizer and native evidence.

The source–factor incidence audit also closes a scientific loophole in MOSAIC. Under optimistic candidate capabilities,
the semantic-axis matrix is rank 5 of 7 and the source-adjusted matrix is rank 8 of 12 because modality and specimen are
paired. The portfolio therefore fails structurally before data admission; more records in the same cells cannot fix it.
Headline scores are unchanged because finding the flaw improves audit quality, not the method or evidence.

Base-conditional preservation is now bounded as a post-B2 competition ablation. Its upside/readiness are 8.4/6.0 and
method novelty is 2.5 because S3FT, positive-congruent/margin training, pointwise/chance constraints, and distillation
own the central mechanism. It can
reduce avoidable regression, but it cannot raise the novelty headline or replace an executed B2 baseline.

Fixed [weight interpolation](WEIGHT_INTERPOLATION_BRANCH.md) is also post-B2. Its prospective competition upside is
8.0 and method novelty is 1.5. Only the predeclared `0.60/0.80/1.00` effective-delta points are eligible, and export
equivalence plus a fresh offline load are mandatory.

## Conservative no-new-evidence ceilings

These are working ceilings under the current evidence class, not promises or mathematical upper bounds. “No new
evidence” means no evidence beyond the October 23 repository state: the October 14 organizer reply, current source
audits, remote CI, and the bounded B1 and B2-P native synthetic receipts. It excludes a new organizer answer, admitted
real data, answer/context objective execution, full training, or a biomedical experiment result.
More prose cannot legitimately cross these ceilings.

The last column states promotion eligibility, not an executable decision rule. Any novelty or idea score increase from
results additionally requires a claim-specific prospective decision object that predates those outputs and freezes the
primary estimand, independent unit, comparator, minimum worthwhile effect or noninferiority floor, uncertainty and
resampling method, multiplicity rule, replication count and independence definition, missing-data behavior, and exact
conjunctive pass/fail function.
