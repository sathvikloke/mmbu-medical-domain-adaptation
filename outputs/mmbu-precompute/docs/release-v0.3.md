# Version 0.3 release notes

This release completes the useful CPU preparation identified by the current research plan and its independent coverage audit. It does not complete the real-model portion of E00 or any E01–E05 result. The remaining critical steps require working allocated compute, authoritative organizer policy, permitted data and suitable biomedical review.

New executable components cover experiment records and confirmation receipts; fixed-mixture score diagnostics and judge-calibration accounting; bounded public ZIP-member acquisition; strict ROCO metadata selection; image presentation; assistant masking and padding; provisional task/context target serialization; and conservative near-duplicate review candidates. Standalone tools provide native processor probes, source-image encoding checks, synthetic causal-loss arithmetic, reproducible ROCO sample preparation and read-only Einstein diagnostics.

The separate local ROCO intake now supplies 24 pending image-backed records across six modalities and 24 articles. Every selected source JPEG has a CRC/hash receipt and preserved bytes; all prepared images retain their native coordinate frame. Article JATS identifies explicit CC BY versions for all 24 source articles and unique exact caption-to-figure links for 22. These findings do not override ROCO's aggregate noncommercial license, individual-figure exceptions or unresolved model-use eligibility. Article-level overlap with PMC-VQA is substantial and is now recorded in the source registry.

The three cached native processors completed the synthetic input checks and the actual 24-image sample under native and bounded-area settings. Their CPU encodings are evidence about tensors and token counts only. The Qwen3.5 default-thinking prefix mismatch and all three processors' unsafe direct native16 handling are explicitly documented. The loss probe demonstrates why unequal microbatches must normalize over the total shifted assistant-target tokens rather than average their means.

Independent reviews found and closed concrete defects during development:

- An optional tokenizer/config file could escape snapshot-hash verification; exact manifest/file closure is now required.
- ZIP path aliases and case-insensitive collisions could map two selected members to one output. Canonical paths and normalized collision checks now reject them. Range-reader response framing and byte limits also gained adversarial checks.
- Padding could count a first-position label that causal shifting discards. It now requires a masked initial position and at least one usable shifted target.
- Confirmation mutations needed both the independently retained freeze digest and current latest-event digest; every mutation now requires both.
- ROCO sample preparation originally checked member bytes but not contradictory receipt identity declarations. It now rejects mismatched source/version/partition/selection declarations before publishing.
- Perceptual triage could mis-handle the inclusive aspect-ratio boundary and could fingerprint changed bytes after inspection. Integer cross-products and a bounded hash-bound immutable decode close those cases.
- The source-processor probe's bounded reads and finite JSON handling were strengthened after review.

The first ROCO range acquisition timed out. The second bounded attempt succeeded; neither attempt is described as whole-archive checksum verification. Initial failing tests and counterexamples remain in `evidence/` alongside their closure reports. Final source hashes, full-suite logs and clean archive verification distinguish current evidence from earlier snapshots.

Historical 0.1 and 0.2 archives/receipts remain unchanged. Raw biomedical images, metadata caches, virtual environments and tokenizer artifacts are not redistributed in this portable bundle. Their URLs, pins, individual receipts and local reproduction paths are documented separately.
